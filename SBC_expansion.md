# SBC-specific expansion

You might be looking to get more out of your Snowdive by pairing it with a specific SBC that offers extra expansion options.
Here are your extra interfae options for ZERO-compatible SBCs:

## SBCs with PCIe expansion options:

- Radxa Cubie A7Z (FFC)

## SBCs with a camera interface:

All of these use an FFC connector.

- Raspberry Pi Zero 1 (v1.3)/1W/2W (CSI)
- Radxa Zero 3W (CSI)
- Banana Pi M2 Zero (parallel camera interface, obscure)

## SBCs with a DSI interface:

This allows for easily using higher-resolution displays on Snowdive. All of these use an FFC connector.

- MangoPi MQ-Pro
- Luckfox Lyra Zero W

## SBCs with an onboard Ethernet interface:

All of these require you to add a magjack.

- Radxa ROCK Pi S0
- Orange Pi 2W (FFC)
- Walnut Pi Zero (FFC)
- Banana Pi M4 Zero (FFC)

## SBCs with eMMC expansion options:

All of these have soldered-on eMMC option.

- Radxa Cubie A7Z
- Radxa ROCK Pi S0
- Radxa Zero 3W
- Radxa Zero 1W
- Banana Pi M4 Zero
