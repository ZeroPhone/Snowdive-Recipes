# Snowdive-Recipes

Just a sample of things you can do with Snowdive hardware!

- [Expansion port](Expansion.md) at the top of the Snowdive CPU board
- [Neopixel LEDs](Neopixel.md) on Snowdive top and bottom boards
- [QWIIC](QWIIC.md) ports found on Snowdive
- [Display](Displays.md) hacking (display types, options, hackability)
- [SBC-specific expansion](SBC_expansion.md): DSI, PCIe, Ethernet, and more!
- [UART addons](UART_addons.md) (with a GPS addon as an example)
- [TPM](TPM.md) chip footprint found on the Snowdive top board
- [Addon board](Addon.md) FFC interface, used by the `palmtop` addon, usable by many other purposes
- [Palmtop base](Palmtop.md) expansion interfaces
- [SDIO](SDIO.md) hacking - ESP32-C5/C6 connection, or other SDIO addons (microSD/eMMC/etc.)
- [USB-PD](USB-PD.md) interfaces, available both on the Snowdive CPU board and the `palmtop` addon board
